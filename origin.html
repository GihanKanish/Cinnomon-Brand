We're a small, founder-led export house in Kandy, Sri Lanka — where soil and humidity create conditions for true Cinnamomum verum that can't be replicated anywhere else.

The place

Sri Lanka's Kandy region and Central Province.

The cinnamon belt runs from Negombo south through Gampaha, Kalutara, Galle, and Matara. The same narrow strip of coast has grown Cinnamomum verum for over 500 years. No other region on earth produces the same combination of aroma compounds — a result of the specific soil chemistry, humidity range, and centuries of cultivar selection.

Ceylon Cinnamon received EU Protected Geographical Indication status in 2022. PGI means the spice can only carry the name if it genuinely originates in Sri Lanka. We intend to be worthy of that designation.
